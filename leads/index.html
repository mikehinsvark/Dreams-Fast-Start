The DREAMS Framework · D — R — E — A — M — S

One organized view of opportunity areas.

The framework helps a rep connect public business context to relevant specialist conversations. It does not establish eligibility, a savings result, a funding offer, or a client recommendation.

D

Debt & Funding

Funding

Organize an internal conversation about funding needs and options without making an offer.

R

Retirement

Savings

Identify whether a specialist conversation around retirement readiness may be appropriate.

E

Expenses

Savings

Frame observable operating context for a disciplined expense-review conversation.

A

Assets

Profit

Consider how business assets may connect to a planning conversation; do not infer value.

M

Money & Credits

Profit

Surface questions around cash flow and available credits only when supported by the review.

S

Security

Savings

Prepare a careful conversation around benefits, protection, and business continuity context.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about Intent Data and lead distribution.

Is this a public lead directory?

No. Intent Data is a protected beta workflow for approved release campaigns only.

Can records be re-claimed?

No. A successful exclusive claim removes that record from the available pool.

Are contact records fabricated?

No. This destination starts without seeded contact data and does not manufacture personal contact records.

Is payment active?

No. Payment and all third-party outbound integrations remain disabled and configuration-only.

Can I search multiple areas at once?

Only within the allowed filters of a currently released campaign. The available controls are set by the campaign release.

What happens when no campaigns are released?

The portal shows its empty state. It does not create or display synthetic campaign or contact data.
