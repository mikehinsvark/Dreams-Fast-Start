# Dreams AI and Specialist Handoff Playbook

Version: 2.0  
Updated: September 23, 2026

## Start with the tools you already have

One AI conversation, a phone, and a notebook or simple tracker are enough to begin. The agent makes decisions and performs outreach, or explicitly authorizes an available tool. Additional apps are optional; they are never a prerequisite for the first useful conversation.

The Executive Partner is the depth and continuity of coaching. The five Genie roles are **Coach, Onboarding, Sales, Intelligence, and Partnership**, working from one shared coaching context. They are not independent background agents. An “Executive Partner” is not a sixth Genie.

No shared context is implied between separate apps, chats, uploaded files, or accounts. The user carries the necessary context between them unless a real authorized connection exists. A proposed action, copied prompt, or draft message does not prove anything ran or was sent.

## Two different kinds of handoff

- **Specialist handoff:** a consenting prospect is introduced to the appropriate human specialist through the confirmed Dreams/provider route.
- **AI handoff:** the user gives a bounded preparation task and necessary context to an available tool, then brings its result back for review.

An AI handoff does not refer a client, book a specialist, enroll anyone, or authorize contact.

## A. Introduce a prospect to a human specialist

Before submitting client details, confirm the receiving specialist, current approved route, permissions, and who owns follow-up. Use the client's stated need; the specialist determines fit, eligibility, technical answers, and terms.

1. Ask the prospect whether they want the introduction and what contact information may be shared.
2. Confirm the actual referral or booking route with the sponsor. Do not invent links, availability, or contacts.
3. Introduce the person with the minimum necessary facts. Send sensitive records only through the approved secure intake route.
4. Record the actual submission and whether the specialist acknowledged receipt. These are separate events.
5. Confirm any meeting details and follow up at the agreed time. Record the next action, owner, and due time.

### Copy-ready introduction

> [Prospect], with your permission, I am introducing you to [confirmed specialist] about [your stated business need]. [Specialist], [prospect] would like to explore [agreed purpose]. The proposed next step is [specific step]. Please confirm who will coordinate it and when. [Agent] will stay connected for follow-up.

Replace every bracket with confirmed information. Share the message only with permission through the agreed channel. Proposed times stay proposed until the required participants confirm.

### Find the current route

Public starting points checked September 23, 2026:

- [21-Day Fast Start](https://dreamsfaststart.com/21days/) and [training hub](https://dreamsfaststart.com/) for orientation; ask the sponsor for the exact approved lesson.
- [Dreams Back Office](https://dreams.poweredagency.com/) through the agent's own access.

These are not verified specialist booking or submission links. **Confirm with your sponsor before referring.**

Sponsor request: “I am preparing an introduction about [need]. Who is the current receiving specialist, what approved referral or booking route should I use, and who owns follow-up? Please send the current instructions.”

If the route is missing, continue practice and non-sensitive preparation. Mark it “pending confirmation.” If the sponsor is unavailable, use a support route actually shown in the Back Office or ask a known Dreams leader. Never invent an answer to keep the process moving.

## B. Prepare work with an AI role or another tool

Use the current conversation when it can handle the work. Choose a role by the help needed:

| Need | Genie role | Small, useful output |
|---|---|---|
| Choose today's priority or recover after a missed action | Coach | One Next Meaningful Action and a realistic due time |
| Understand or practice a new step | Onboarding | Demonstration, supported retry, independent try |
| Prepare outreach, discovery, or a specialist introduction | Sales | A short draft or role-play and one improvement |
| Check a prospect fact or current source | Intelligence | Facts, assumptions, source dates, and questions to verify |
| Explore a strategic or channel partner | Partnership | Mutual value, roles, and a small agreed test |

A different app may be useful for a particular document, source collection, or workflow. ChatGPT, Claude/Cowork, NotebookLM, or another tool may be options the user already has. Select by demonstrated capability, access, and task requirements—not a fixed brand assignment. Do not promise that an app can edit files, monitor work, send messages, or run in the background without checking the actual tool and permissions. The same condition applies to any proposed recurring-operation tool, including Grokbot.

### Simple AI handoff packet

```text
OUTCOME: [one useful result]
CONTEXT: [only the goal, facts, and decisions needed]
INPUTS: [the supplied text, files, or verified links]
DELIVERABLE: [a short draft, checklist, analysis, or file; what done means]
BOUNDARIES: Follow the Dreams referral model. Keep facts separate from assumptions.
Do not invent claims, contacts, access, approvals, or completed actions.
Use minimum necessary information. No external action is authorized by this packet.
RETURN: The result; unresolved questions; one recommended next action.
```

The coach can fill this in for a beginner. The user opens the available tool, supplies the packet and permitted inputs, and returns the result. Manual copy and paste is a valid workflow. If the tool is unavailable or confusing, finish the preparation in the current conversation or use a simple note.

### Bring the result back

> Review this result against my Dreams Core Memory and current goal. Tell me what is usable, what needs revision or verification, and the next action I can complete. Give me a brief proposed journal update only if there is a durable lesson or actual result.

Review the result, not the tool's confidence. For time-sensitive program claims, use current official sources and record the title, owner, and date. Ask the responsible Dreams leader or provider to resolve conflicts. Neither an AI response nor this playbook approves a program claim.

## Close every handoff with an honest status

Record **Person / Action / Owner / Due / Done**. Use the actual state: drafted, sent, submitted, acknowledged, proposed, confirmed, or held. A specialist acknowledgment does not mean a sale, approval, enrollment, or payment. An AI response does not mean an external action happened.

At the next check-in ask **Done / Tried / Not started**. Record the real result, resolve one blocker, and choose the next useful step. Keep private records in their proper system. Proposed profile or journal updates are not saved until the user saves them or an authorized tool verifies the write.
