# Dreams Coach — Start Here

Version: 2.0  
Updated: September 23, 2026

**Your first win: practice one simple conversation and choose one useful action you can complete today.** You do not need to learn the whole Dreams business or set up several AI tools first.

Your job is to start a helpful conversation, earn permission for an introduction, and connect the person with the appropriate specialist. The specialist explains the program, determines fit, and handles technical questions and service.

## 1. Begin now in one chat

1. Open ChatGPT and start a conversation. Use an account you can access; this guide does not require a paid plan or another AI app to begin. Available features can vary.
2. Copy the message below, paste it into the conversation, and send it. No file uploads are needed for this first practice.
3. Answer one question at a time. Typing is enough. If Voice is available to you and you prefer it, you can speak instead.

### Copy this first message

```text
Be my Dreams beginner coach. I have little or no technical experience. Use plain words, short steps, and one question at a time. Do not ask me to complete a long profile or set up more tools before helping me practice.

Dreams starter context: My role is to identify a possible business need, start a permission-based conversation, arrange an appropriate specialist introduction, and follow up. The specialist explains current programs, decides fit or eligibility, handles technical questions, and provides service. I must not promise earnings, savings, eligibility, approval, funding, benefits, or results.

The starter learning order is Week 1: R&D tax-credit review; Week 2: business funding, called Bank Breezy in this pack; Week 3: an employer benefits review, called Quantum Health in this pack. These are learning topics, not confirmation that a program is currently available or suitable. Verify current claims and the referral route with official Dreams/provider material or my sponsor. Do not invent amounts, criteria, terms, or results.

Start by asking how much time I have today. Then ask whether I am on a phone or computer and how comfortable I am with it. Then ask what kinds of business owners or professional contacts I already know. My name is optional; do not request anyone's private information. Ask about my sponsor only if help or referral-route confirmation is needed.

Give me one short example, such as: “I help business owners connect with specialists for a review of a possible business need. Would you be open to a brief conversation to see whether an introduction would be useful?” Adapt it to the current topic without adding unverified claims. Role-play a short conversation with me, give one strength and one improvement, and let me retry.

End with one Next Meaningful Action: who it is for (an alias or audience is enough), one task, who will do it (me by default), the due time, and an observable sign it is done. I will report back Done, Tried, or Not started, plus what happened. Done means the original done criterion was met; Tried means I started but did not meet it; Not started means I have not begun. Report any reply or business result separately. If my task was to send an invitation and I sent it, it is Done even without a reply. If I need an approved introduction route, help me ask my sponsor for it. On my return, review that result and choose what comes next. A sent invitation is not a conversation or a held appointment. If I miss a day, restart from today without a catch-up burden.

Use one shared context with five roles: Coach, Onboarding, Sales, Intelligence, and Partnership. Coach leads. These are ways of helping in this conversation, not separate background agents. Greater Executive Partner depth comes as my skills and needs grow.

Do not claim to see files I have not provided, save changes to my files, remember across chats, monitor activity, or send anything unless the capability exists and the action is verified. Draft a short progress note I can save or paste into my next chat. Start with the first question now.
```

## 2. Take your Next Meaningful Action

Keep it small enough for the time you have. Examples:

- Practice one opening aloud and retry it using your coach's feedback.
- Ask your sponsor to confirm the current specialist introduction route.
- After checking the route, send one permission-based invitation to a relevant contact.

Before you leave, complete this sentence:

> For [person or audience; an alias is fine], I will [one task] by [time]. It is done when [observable result]. I will report back Done, Tried, or Not started, plus what happened.

You own the action unless you and the coach explicitly name another owner. For a practice task, the audience can be yourself or a practice partner.

**Done** means you met the original done criterion. **Tried** means you started but did not meet it. **Not started** means you have not begun. Keep the other person's response separate: if the task was to send an invitation and you sent it, mark Done even if no reply has arrived.

When you return, type:

> My action is [Done / Tried / Not started]. I did ___. What happened was ___. Help me choose my next meaningful action.

Missed a day? Type:

> I am restarting today. I have ___ minutes. Start from my last real action and help me take one useful next step.

## 3. Set up your continuing coach when you are ready

This setup gives the coach your approved reference material and saved progress. It can wait until after your first practice.

1. Download this pack. If it is a ZIP file, open or extract it. The four files are inside **AGENT-READY**. Ask your sponsor for help if you cannot find them.
2. In ChatGPT, create a project if that feature is available to your account. You could name it **My Dreams Coach**. Exact controls vary; see the [official Projects guide](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/projects).
3. Add these four files using the project's **Sources/files** area or the equivalent shown on your screen:
   - `01-DREAMS-EXECUTIVE-PARTNER-CORE-MEMORY.md`
   - `02-MY-DREAMS-OPERATING-PROFILE.md`
   - `03-DREAMS-MEMORY-JOURNAL.md`
   - `04-AI-HANDOFF-PLAYBOOK.md`
4. Open `PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS.txt` in the pack. Copy its contents into the project's instructions area where shown. If you cannot find that area, paste the contents into a conversation in the project and ask your sponsor to help finish setup later.
5. Send: “List which of the four Dreams files you can actually read. Tell me if one is missing. Use the available context, ask one question at a time, and help me continue from my last action.”

If project features are unavailable, continue in one chat with the begin-now message and the short progress note below. You can add the reference files to a chat if your account supports it. A coach should identify missing context without blocking basic practice.

## 4. Save progress without assuming automatic updates

The coach can draft an update. That does **not** mean your downloaded file or uploaded copy changed.

1. Ask: “Draft my progress update. Show me exactly what to save.”
2. Check that it contains only facts you supplied or agreed to. Correct anything uncertain.
3. Copy the update into your saved Profile or Journal file. If the coach provides a downloadable replacement, review and download it instead. Keep the same filename.
4. Replace the older uploaded copy in your project. Ask the coach to read back the update date and last action from the new copy. If it cannot, keep the update in the conversation and ask for help.

**Easy fallback:** copy this into a note on your phone or computer, then paste it into your next chat:

```text
MY DREAMS RESUME NOTE — [date]
Current week/topic:
Last action I actually completed:
What happened:
Next action: [person/audience; one task; owner (me by default); due time; done means]
Report back: [Done / Tried / Not started; what happened]
Specialist introduction route: [confirmed source/date, or not yet verified]
Help I need:
```

Use initials or business categories. Leave out passwords, account details, tax or health records, and private client information.

## 5. Get human help when you need it

Send this yourself to your sponsor or team support contact:

> I am starting Dreams and need a little help with [step]. I am using a [phone/computer]. I can get as far as [what I see], but I need help with [next step]. Can you walk me through it and confirm the current specialist introduction route for this week's topic?

You are making progress when you can explain your role, use a clear opening, ask permission for the right introduction, follow up, and report real outcomes. The 21-day outline is a guide; your coach should help you build those skills at your pace.
