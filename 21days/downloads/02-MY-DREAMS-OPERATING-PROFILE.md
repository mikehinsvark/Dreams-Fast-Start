## How this file stays current

1. Ask the coach to draft the exact update and show it to you.
2. Check the facts. Update **My last saved update** to the save date. Save this file, or review and download a replacement containing that date if offered.
3. Replace the older uploaded copy in your project.
4. Ask the coach to read back **My last saved update** and **Last action I actually completed** from that copy. Until verified, treat the update as a draft or chat note.

The chat, downloaded file, and project upload are separate copies. They do not silently synchronize. If saving feels difficult, ask for a short **My Dreams Resume Note** to copy into your next chat.

Only include information you are comfortable providing as AI context. Do not add passwords, account numbers, identity documents, private client details, or tax, payroll, banking, or health records. The coach must ask before adding a new durable personal detail that you have not already agreed to save.
