## My simple daily entry

### [Date]

**I did:** [Done / Tried / Not started; the action I actually took]

**What happened:** [the real response or outcome; remove private details]

**My next meaningful action:** For [person or audience; an alias is fine], I will [one task] by [time]. It is done when [observable result]. I will report back Done, Tried, or Not started, plus what happened.

I own my next action unless we explicitly agree on another owner and record that person's name or alias. For solo practice, the audience can be myself.

**Done** means I met the original done criterion. **Tried** means I started but did not meet it. **Not started** means I have not begun. The reply or business result goes under **What happened**. If my task was to send an invitation and I sent it, I mark Done even if no reply has arrived.

## Save and check

1. Ask: “Draft these three lines for my journal.”
2. Read the draft and correct it. An AI suggestion is not something you actually did.
3. Paste it under **My entries** in your saved copy of this file. If your coach offers a downloadable replacement, review and download it instead. Update **My last saved entry**.
4. Replace the older uploaded copy in your project, then ask the coach to read back the newest entry date and next action. If it cannot, the upload is not verified.

Your coach has not changed a downloaded file merely by writing a journal entry in chat. If you cannot update the file yet, save the three lines in a phone note and paste them into the next conversation.

## If I miss a day

Say: “I am back. My last completed action was ___. I have ___ minutes today. Help me restart with one useful action.”

Record today honestly. Do not invent past activity or create extra work to catch up with a streak.

## Keep the useful parts

Save a tested sentence, a repeated objection, a verified lesson, or a commitment when it will improve your next session. Label suggestions and assumptions clearly. Do not save a full transcript by default.

Use initials or business categories. Leave out passwords, identity documents, account details, private client information, and tax, payroll, banking, or health records.
