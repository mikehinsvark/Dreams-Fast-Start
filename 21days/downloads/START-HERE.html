DREAMS COACH · VERSION 2.0 · SEPTEMBER 23, 2026

Start with one conversation.

Your first win: practice a simple opening and choose one useful action for today. Typing is enough. Voice is optional if available.

1. Begin now

  1. Open ChatGPT and start a new conversation.
  2. Copy the starter below, paste it into your chat, and send it.
  3. Answer one question at a time. Your coach will show you an example and help you practice.

No file setup or paid plan is required to begin. Features vary by account.

2. Take one useful action

Decide with your coach: who, one action, who will do it, when, and what counts as done.

By [time], I will [action] for [person or audience]. It is done when [observable result]. I will report back Done, Tried, or Not started, and what happened.

Your role is to start the conversation, ask permission for an introduction, and follow up. Specialists explain programs, check fit, and handle technical questions.

If you need a specialist route, ask your sponsor to confirm it before making a live referral. No answer from a contact is an outcome; it does not undo an action you completed.

3. Add reference files when ready

You can keep practicing in one chat. If project features are available, use them for your continuing coach.

  1. Create a project called My Dreams Coach. Exact controls vary; see the official Projects guide.
  2. Add the four numbered files in AGENT-READY (01–04) to the project's Sources/files area. Do not upload the ZIP as a substitute for the four files.
  3. Copy PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS.txt into the instructions area where available. If you cannot find it, paste the instructions into a project conversation and ask your sponsor for help.
  4. Send: “List which of the four Dreams files you can actually read. Tell me if one is missing. Ask one question at a time and help me continue from my last action.”
Open the project instructions

You do not have to fill in the whole profile before practicing.

4. Come back without starting over

Ask your coach for a short resume note: last real action, outcome, next action, due time, and help needed. Save it in a note on your device and paste it into your next chat.

For continuing files: review the coach's draft update, save it in your Profile or Journal with an update date, replace the older project upload, and ask the coach to read back the date and last action.

The chat, downloaded file, and project upload are separate copies. They do not automatically synchronize.

Missed a day? Say: “I am restarting today. I have ___ minutes. Help me take one useful next step.”

Need a person to help?

Send your sponsor: “I need help with [step]. I am using a [phone/computer]. Can you walk me through it and confirm this week's specialist introduction route?”

Use contact initials or categories in AI notes. Leave out passwords and private client records.
